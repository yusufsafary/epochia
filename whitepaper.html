Mining, minus the friction挖礦，減去所有阻力

Why Agent Browser existsAgent Browser 為何存在

Agent mining projects usually ask users to pay again and again: per share, per boost, per entry. Each payment is a chance to hesitate, misread a price, or give up. Agent Browser removes the repeat payments entirely.代理挖礦項目通常要求用戶一再付款：逐份、逐次加速、逐次入場。每次付款都是一次猶豫、誤讀價格或放棄的機會。Agent Browser 徹底移除重複付款。 There is exactly one purchase in the whole system: the Browser Key.整個系統只有一次購買：Browser Key。 Everything after that is a harvest, not a checkout.之後的一切都是收穫，不是結帳。

Key鑰匙

The Browser Key is a non-fungible token on BNB Chain, priced at $0.50 in BNB, capped at 10,000 keys and at most 10 keys per wallet. Minting runs only through a Binance Agentic Wallet agent, and the minting wallet must hold a Binance Agent ID. The key is transferable: whoever holds the key holds its future harvest, and claims already collected stay with whoever collected them.Browser Key 是 BNB 鏈上的非同質化代幣，價格為 $0.50 的 BNB，總上限 10,000 把，每個錢包最多 10 把。鑄造只經由 Binance Agentic Wallet 代理進行，鑄造錢包必須持有 Binance Agent ID。鑰匙可以轉讓：持有鑰匙的人擁有其未來收穫，已領取的部分則歸領取者所有。

Harvest收穫

  • One key, one claim per day.一把鑰匙，每日一次領取。 Claims accrue per key from the day after the key is minted.領取由鑰匙鑄造翌日起逐日累積。
  • Claims stack.領取可累積。 Uncollected days accumulate and are paid in one call, at the rates of the days they belong to.未領取的日子會累積起來，以一次呼叫按各日所屬比率支付。
  • No claim fee.領取不收費用。 Harvesting moves only BROWSE. The network gas is the only cost.收穫只轉移 BROWSE，網絡 gas 是唯一成本。
  • Counting is global.計數是全網的。 Every claim paid by the contract, to anyone, advances the shared claim counter.合約支付的每一次領取，無論對象是誰，都會推進共享的領取計數。

Epochs and halving週期與減半

Agent Browser borrows its clock from Bitcoin and swaps blocks for claims. Every 150,000 claims paid network-wide, the per-claim amount halves. There are eleven epochs. When Epoch 11 ends, harvesting ends with it, and any BROWSE left in the mining reserve is never created into circulation.Agent Browser 借用比特幣的時鐘，把區塊換成領取次數。全網每支付 150,000 次領取，每次領取的金額便減半。週期共有十一個。Epoch 11 結束時，收穫亦隨之結束，挖礦儲備中剩餘的 BROWSE 永遠不會進入流通。

Where the money goes資金流向

Key sales are the project's only direct revenue. They accumulate in the key contract as BNB, are withdrawable by the project, and seed the initial BROWSE liquidity pool on PancakeSwap at the token launch. The full allocation is on the鑰匙銷售是項目唯一的直接收入。收入以 BNB 累積在鑰匙合約內，可由項目提取，並於代幣上線時為 PancakeSwap 的 BROWSE 初始流動池提供種子資金。完整分配見 tokenomics page代幣經濟頁面.

Honest limits誠實的界限

BROWSE begins with no market price; the pool sets one at launch and it can move sharply either way. A key guarantees a harvest schedule, not a dollar value. If few keys sell, epochs simply run longer in calendar time, because the halving counts claims, not days.BROWSE 起始時沒有市場價格；價格由上線時的流動池定出，可向任一方向大幅波動。鑰匙保證的是收穫時間表，不是美元價值。若鑰匙銷售緩慢，週期在日曆時間上只會拉長，因為減半數的是領取次數，不是日子。
