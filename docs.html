  • Do claims expire?領取會過期嗎？ No. Uncollected days wait for you. But once Epoch 11 ends, no new claims accrue and unharvested reserve is never paid.不會。未領取的日子會等你。但 Epoch 11 結束後，不再產生新領取，未收穫的儲備永遠不會支付。
  • Can I sell a key?可以出售鑰匙嗎？ Yes, keys are standard NFTs and trade on any marketplace. Future harvest follows the key; past harvest stays with whoever claimed it.可以。鑰匙是標準 NFT，可在任何市場交易。未來收穫跟隨鑰匙；已領取的收穫歸領取者。
