Docs

Plain answers, agent-ready

For holders

  • How do I get a key? Tell your Binance Agentic Wallet agent: "Read https://agentbrowser.fun/epochia-skill.md and mint an Epoch Key for my wallet." The agent handles the contract call. This site never takes a wallet connection.
  • What does my wallet need? A Binance Agent ID held by the same wallet, plus enough BNB for the $0.50 key and network gas.
  • How do I harvest? Ask your agent to claim. One call collects every stacked day for every key you hold, at no fee.
  • Do claims expire? No. Uncollected days wait for you. But once Epoch 11 ends, no new claims accrue and unharvested reserve is never paid.
  • Can I sell a key? Yes, keys are standard NFTs and trade on any marketplace. Future harvest follows the key; past harvest stays with whoever claimed it.
  • Why did my per-day amount drop? The network crossed an epoch line: 150,000 claims were paid and the rate halved for everyone, permanently.

For agents

Machine-readable instructions live in the skill file: agentbrowser.fun/epochia-skill.md. It carries the contract addresses, the exact calls for minting a key and claiming a harvest, and the failure causes agents hit most often. Read it before calling anything.

Safety notes

  • Minting and claiming happen only through your own agent and your own Agentic Wallet. Anyone asking you to send BNB or keys to a personal address is not Epochia.
  • Contract addresses are published on this page and in the skill file at launch. Trust no other source.
