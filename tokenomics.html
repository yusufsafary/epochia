Tokenomics

21 billion EPO. Fixed forever.

EPO is minted once, in full, at deployment. No function can create more. The mining reserve pays harvests; it is not a promise to print.

  • Mining reserve: 12,600,000,000 EPO sits in the harvest contract and pays daily claims across the eleven epochs.
  • Initial pool: 4,200,000,000 EPO pairs with BNB from key sales when the PancakeSwap pool opens at token launch.
  • Liquidity support: 2,100,000,000 EPO is held back for deepening the pool as volume grows.
  • Project reserve: 2,100,000,000 EPO covers operations, listings and future incentives, held by the project wallet.

Key economics

  • Epoch Key price: $0.50 in BNB, locked in wei at deployment. 10,000 keys maximum, 10 per wallet.
  • Sold out, key sales total roughly $5,000 in BNB before any trading exists. That BNB seeds the pool and funds operations.
  • Harvest claims carry no fee. The project earns from key sales and a planned 1% trade tax on pool swaps, half of Epochia's long-term operating income.

Emission check

Epoch 1 pays 42,000 EPO per claim and each epoch pays half the previous one across 150,000 claims. Summed over eleven epochs the schedule pays about 12.59 billion EPO, just under the 12.6 billion reserve. The small remainder stays locked in the harvest contract when the mine closes.
