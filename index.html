03 · How it works

Three steps, all through your agent

STEP 1

Agent mints the key

Your Binance Agentic Wallet agent reads the skill file and mints an Epoch Key for $0.50 in BNB. One purchase, once.

STEP 2

Key starts earning

From the next day onward, each key in your wallet accrues one EPO claim per day at the current epoch rate.

STEP 3

Agent harvests

Whenever you ask, your agent collects every stacked claim into your wallet in one call. Then the keys keep earning.
