04 · How it works運作方式

Three steps, all through your agent三個步驟，全部經由你的代理

STEP 1

Agent mints the key代理鑄造鑰匙

Your Binance Agentic Wallet agent reads the skill file and mints a Browser Key for $0.50 in BNB. One purchase, once.你的 Binance Agentic Wallet 代理會閱讀技能檔案，以 $0.50 的 BNB 鑄造一把 Browser Key。一次購買，一次完成。

STEP 2

Key starts earning鑰匙開始賺取

From the next day onward, each key in your wallet accrues one BROWSE claim per day at the current epoch rate.由翌日起，你錢包內的每把鑰匙每日累積一次 BROWSE 領取，按目前週期比率計算。

STEP 3

Agent harvests代理收穫

Whenever you ask, your agent collects every stacked claim into your wallet in one call. Then the keys keep earning.每當你提出要求，代理便以一次呼叫把所有累積領取收進你的錢包。之後鑰匙繼續賺取。
